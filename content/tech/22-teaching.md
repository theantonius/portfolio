I treat curriculum design as a creative practice, building courses around hands-on making with technical skills embedded in meaningful projects. I design and teach courses in creative coding, web development, e-textiles, physical computing, and interactive media at NYU, CUNY, and NYC FIRST STEM Centers. I believe the best technical education is grounded in curiosity, and I design learning experiences that make room for experimentation and productive failure.
